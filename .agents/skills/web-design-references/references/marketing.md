# Marketing pages and expressive sections

Use section libraries to solve hierarchy and composition. Choose motion only when it supports the message; the [motion decision guide](motion.md#emil-kowalski) applies when an effect is being considered.

## Shadcnblocks

**Keywords:** landing page, hero, pricing, testimonials, feature section, ecommerce, page composition.

**Observed:** [Shadcnblocks](https://www.shadcnblocks.com/) organizes React, Tailwind, and shadcn/ui examples into sections, components, and templates. Categories include heroes, features, pricing, social proof, ecommerce, and application layouts. It offers different primitive implementations and both free and paid content.

**Apply:** Use for the structure of a page or substantial section. Compare examples for the actual content shape: long benefit copy, several pricing tiers, a product screenshot, or a clear primary action. Extract section order, width relationships, spacing cadence, and responsive grouping; apply the project's own identity and content.

**Limits / access:** Browse the relevant category instead of the whole catalog. Some source requires an account or purchase. Check the chosen block's primitive variant and dependencies. A visually complete template still needs real navigation, forms, content, and product behavior.

## Magic UI

**Keywords:** animated accent, bento, marquee, product demo, text effect, SaaS, portfolio.

**Observed:** [Magic UI](https://magicui.design/) offers animated React components and effects using TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Motion. Its [component index](https://magicui.design/docs/components) includes marquees, bento grids, hero video dialogs, animated lists, docks, globes, and orbiting compositions. Pro templates are offered separately.

**Apply:** Use when an otherwise coherent page needs a focal feature or compact visual explanation. A bento can organize distinct capabilities; an animated list can illustrate a changing workflow. Choose the component because it communicates the content, then adjust its rhythm and styling to the surrounding page.

**Limits / access:** Inspect the selected component's dependencies and source. Continuous movement can compete with reading and consume resources; verify legibility, reduced motion, and off-screen behavior. Free components do not imply that all templates or bundled assets are free.

## Aceternity UI

**Keywords:** expressive hero, spotlight, parallax, hover, 3D card, background, reveal.

**Observed:** [Aceternity UI](https://ui.aceternity.com/) has [React component examples](https://ui.aceternity.com/components) built with Tailwind and Motion, including spotlight backgrounds, parallax, card effects, text reveals, navigation, and full sections. Free examples and paid blocks/templates are distinguished in the catalog.

**Apply:** Use when the brief calls for a visually expressive moment, such as a product reveal or interactive portfolio card. Study depth, layering, image treatment, and the relation between the trigger and its response. Keep the page's main message readable before and during the effect.

**Limits / access:** Check each example's actual dependencies; effect implementations vary. Pointer-driven examples need a usable touch and keyboard adaptation. Validate scroll behavior, resource cost, and static presentation before choosing a complex effect for a content-heavy page.

## ReUI

**Keywords:** pricing, contact, comparison, CTA, FAQ, marketing sections.

**Contribution here:** [ReUI's marketing blocks](https://reui.io/blocks/marketing/pricing) cover pricing, comparisons, contact, calls to action, and FAQs. They can inform content and conversion sections alongside its better-known application components.

**Apply:** Enter the marketing category matching the section, then compare how examples organize choices and supporting information. Keep the primary action clear with realistic copy lengths. Check the [free-component versus paid-block distinction](application-components.md#reui) for the selected item.

## Recent

**Keywords:** brand direction, typography, editorial landing page, imagery, portfolio identity.

**Contribution here:** [Recent](https://recent.design/) helps establish a page's visual identity before selecting reusable sections. Website, typography, and branding references can suggest a coherent type hierarchy, image treatment, and spacing rhythm.

**Apply:** Select examples close to the intended audience and tone, then translate the direction into the page's own content. Read the [visual evidence limits](visual-product.md#recent) before treating gallery metadata as visual inspection.

## Canvas UI

**Keywords:** interactive hero, material effect, particle reveal, product showcase.

**Contribution here:** [Canvas UI](https://canvasui.dev/) can provide a focal treatment around product imagery or content, such as a reveal, distortion, or glass effect. Its role here is to support a specific message within the page composition.

**Apply:** Define what the visitor should understand from the effect and keep the heading and call to action usable in the static presentation. Read the [browser, fallback, and license constraints](canvas-effects.md#canvas-ui) before choosing an implementation.
